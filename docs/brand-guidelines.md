# Supermed Pharmacies Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

### Company Overview
**Supermed Pharmacy (Pvt) Ltd** is Bulawayo's leading pharmacy offering quality pharmaceuticals and cosmetics.

### Tagline
"Enhancing Quality of Life Through Super Products, Super Staff and Super Service"

### Brand Values
- **Quality:** Excellence in pharmaceutical products and services
- **Service:** Professional, friendly, and reliable customer service
- **Staff:** Highly trained and knowledgeable pharmacy professionals
- **Trust:** Commitment to health and wellness in the community

## Visual Identity

### Primary Colors
Based on pharmacy industry standards and healthcare aesthetics:
- **Primary Blue:** #2563EB (Professional medical blue)
- **Secondary Green:** #10B981 (Health and wellness)
- **Accent Orange:** #F59E0B (Energy and vitality)
- **Neutral Grays:** #6B7280, #9CA3AF, #E5E7EB (Support colors)

### Typography
- **Headings:** Modern sans-serif fonts (Inter, Roboto, or similar)
- **Body:** Clean, readable sans-serif for maximum legibility
- **Medical emphasis:** Professional medical aesthetics

### Logo Usage
- **Primary Logo:** Located in `public/media/logo.png` (to be uploaded manually)
- **Responsive Scaling:** Automatic resizing across web and mobile
- **Minimum Size:** 32px height for mobile, 48px for desktop
- **Maximum Size:** 200px width for hero sections
- **Background:** Works on both light and dark backgrounds

### Imagery Guidelines
- **Primary Style:** Professional healthcare imagery
- **Secondary Style:** Pharmacy/medical equipment imagery
- **Royalty-Free:** Use stock photography from Unsplash, Pexels, or similar
- **Tone:** Clean, bright, professional, trustworthy
- **Subjects:** Pharmacists, medication, pharmacy interiors, health services

## Voice & Messaging

### Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Professional, caring, knowledgeable, approachable
- **Style:** Clear, concise, friendly, and medically accurate
- **Audience:** Healthcare-conscious consumers, pharmacy customers

### Key Messages
- "Quality pharmaceuticals you can trust"
- "Expert care from trained professionals"
- "Your health is our priority"
- "Super service for super health"

## Application Guidelines

### Web Interface
- **Navigation:** Clean, intuitive, pharmacy-focused
- **Colors:** Primary blue with green accents for health messaging
- **Imagery:** Professional healthcare scenes
- **Typography:** Highly readable for medical information

### Print Materials
- **Business Cards:** Professional pharmacy branding
- **Brochures:** Health-focused imagery with clear typography
- **Signage:** High contrast for readability in store environment

## Asset Organization

### Media Folder Structure
```
public/media/
├── logo.png (primary logo)
├── logo-dark.png (for dark backgrounds)
├── hero-banner.jpg (homepage hero image)
├── pharmacy-interior-1.jpg (store imagery)
├── pharmacist-working.jpg (staff imagery)
└── health-wellness/ (stock imagery library)
```

### Naming Convention
- Use lowercase with hyphens
- Descriptive names for easy identification
- Include dimensions when applicable (e.g., logo-200x200.png)

## Compliance Notes

- Medical accuracy in all health-related content
- Professional pharmacy industry standards
- Zimbabwe healthcare regulations compliance
- Clear disclaimers where appropriate for medical advice
